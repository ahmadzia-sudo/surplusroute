How a collection works

Three steps, and the longest one takes under a minute.

  1. The kitchen lists it

    At closing, staff enter the item, how many portions and whether it is free or discounted. One short form, and it is live.

  2. Someone nearby claims it

    Verified residents see what is available sorted by distance. Claiming holds the food and starts a countdown.

  3. Code at the counter

    They give four characters, take the food and go. Staff see a code and nothing else about the person.

For bakeries, cafes and restaurants

Cheaper than throwing it out

Listing takes seconds and costs your staff nothing at the counter. You decide per item whether to donate it or recover some value, and you set the discount yourself. Collection happens at the front, within a window you control.
